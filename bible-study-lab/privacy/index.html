PRIVACY

Bible Study Lab does not collect, store, sell or share any information about you.

# the short version

Bible Study Lab does not collect, store, sell or share any information about you. There is no account to create, and the app has no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Updated November 1, 2026.

# what stays on your iPhone

Your notes, highlights, reading position, reading-plan progress and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are included in your iPhone's own backups if you have backups turned on, and they are deleted when you delete the app. We never receive them.

# the optional study helper

If you choose to download the AI study helper, the app downloads its model files from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). As with any download, Hugging Face receives your device's IP address and standard request information, under its own privacy policy. The questions you ask and the answers you get are processed entirely on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere.

# reading aloud

Read aloud uses the voices built into iOS, on your iPhone.

# sharing

When you share or export notes, they go only where you send them, such as Messages, Mail or Files.

# children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

# changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new date.
