SUPPORT

Questions, bugs or ideas? We're one email away.

# get help

Email cramirez18444@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can. There is no account to recover, because Brain Kata doesn't use accounts.

To help us fix a problem quickly, include:

  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
  • Your device (for example iPhone 16) and iOS version.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Please leave out anything private.

# common questions

Where is Brain Kata, and is it free?

Brain Kata is in development and isn't on the App Store yet. This page will list the App Store link and pricing when it launches.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. We don't have your decks or your history. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up or move my cards?

Use the share and export options in the app to save your collection to Files or send it to another device. Your normal iPhone backup also keeps the app's data. Syncing between devices isn't available yet.

The camera or microphone isn't working.

Open iOS Settings, find Brain Kata, and make sure Camera and Microphone are turned on. Then try again.

How does the scheduler decide when I see a card?

Brain Kata uses the FSRS-6 scheduling algorithm. You recall the answer, reveal it, and rate it Forgot, Hard, Good or Easy, and the app plans the next review from that. The review screen shows the next interval for each rating.

Can I import cards from another app?

Brain Kata can import Anki-style decks. If an import doesn't look right, email us the details and, if you can, a small sample file.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Please email it. We read everything.
