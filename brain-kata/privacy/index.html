PRIVACY

Study data stays on your device unless you choose to export it or enable backup to your own iCloud Drive.

# the short version

The developer does not receive your decks or review history. Study data is stored on your device. In the new beta candidate, optional iCloud Drive backup sends a collection backup to your own Apple account; it is off by default. There is no Brain Kata account, advertising, or third-party tracking or analytics code in the app.

Updated October 7, 2026 to describe the optional iCloud backup in the internal beta candidate. This policy covers Brain Kata and this website.

# who we are

Brain Kata is made by Chris Ramirez, doing business as ADHD (AI Driven Haptic Design). Questions about privacy go to cramirez18444@gmail.com.

# what stays on your device

  • Decks and cards you create or import, including any text, images and audio you attach.
  • Your review history, scheduling data and progress calendar.
  • Your settings and preferences.

This is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. If you enable iCloud backup or export a collection, the backup includes your collection and attached media. Device-local presentation preferences and collected-quote history are not part of the collection backup. We run no app-data servers and do not receive these backups.

# permissions the app may ask for

Brain Kata asks only when you use a feature that needs it, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

Camera

To take a photo or scan document pages so you can turn them into editable flashcard drafts, or to attach a photo to a card. Photos and scans are processed on your device.

Microphone

To record your voice for private pronunciation practice, or to save a recording to a card when you choose. Recordings stay on your device.

Photos you pick

If you choose a picture to add to a card, only the picture you select is used.

# read-aloud and other built-in features

Read-aloud uses the speech voices built into iOS on your device. Brain Kata does not send your card text to any speech or AI service.

# sharing and exporting

You can export or share cards using the standard iOS share sheet. That only happens when you start it, and the destination you choose (for example Messages, Mail or Files) is governed by that app's own privacy practices.

# optional iCloud Drive backup

The new beta candidate includes an opt-in iCloud Drive backup feature. With automatic backup enabled, a changed collection can be backed up at most once per day after study or when the app leaves the foreground. You can also explicitly choose Back up now. Files go to your own iCloud Drive, in Brain Kata → Backups, and use Apple's iCloud service and storage allowance. No copy is sent to the developer.

The app keeps recent backups and prunes only files created by this feature according to its retention policy. Restore verifies a downloaded backup, asks for confirmation and saves a local snapshot before replacing the collection. Live iCloud device verification is still pending for this candidate.

This is backup and restore, not live sync between devices. AI integrations and live multi-device sync are not enabled.

# TestFlight beta testing

Apple's TestFlight service can provide crash logs, usage information and feedback you submit to the developer. This is separate from the app's collection backups. Do not include private card content in feedback or screenshots unless you intend to share it. See Apple's TestFlight information.

# children

Brain Kata is a study tool for general audiences and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

# deleting your data

Turning off automatic iCloud backup stops future automatic backups; it does not delete existing backup files. Exported files and iCloud Drive backups are separate copies under your control and may remain after removing the app. Manage those copies through Files or your Apple storage settings. The developer holds no copy to delete.

# this website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, cookies or trackers of our own. GitHub, as the host, may keep standard server logs under its own privacy statement. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

# changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
