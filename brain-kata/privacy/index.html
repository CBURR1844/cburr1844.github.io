PRIVACY

Plain language, no fine print: Brain Kata keeps your study data on your device.

# the short version

Brain Kata does not collect your data. Your decks, cards, review history and settings stay on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party tracking or analytics code.

Effective October 4, 2026. This policy covers the Brain Kata app for iPhone and iPad and this website.

# who we are

Brain Kata is made by Chris Ramirez, doing business as ADHD (AI Driven Haptic Design). Questions about privacy go to cramirez18444@gmail.com.

# what stays on your device

  • Decks and cards you create or import, including any text, images and audio you attach.
  • Your review history, scheduling data and progress calendar.
  • Your settings and preferences.

All of this is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. We run no servers for the app, so we never receive it and cannot read it, sell it or share it.

# permissions the app may ask for

Brain Kata asks only when you use a feature that needs it, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

Camera

To take a photo or scan document pages so you can turn them into editable flashcard drafts, or to attach a photo to a card. Photos and scans are processed on your device.

Microphone

To record your voice for private pronunciation practice, or to save a recording to a card when you choose. Recordings stay on your device.

Photos you pick

If you choose a picture to add to a card, only the picture you select is used.

# read-aloud and other built-in features

Read-aloud uses the speech voices built into iOS on your device. Brain Kata does not send your card text to any speech or AI service.

# sharing and exporting

You can export or share cards using the standard iOS share sheet. That only happens when you start it, and the destination you choose (for example Messages, Mail or Files) is governed by that app's own privacy practices.

# sync and future features

Syncing between devices is not enabled in the current version. If we add sync, an AI feature or anything else that sends data off your device, we will update this policy first, explain what is sent and why, and ask for your permission in the app before anything is used.

# children

Brain Kata is a study tool for general audiences and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

# deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device removes the data it stores on that device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing else for us to delete. If you used the share sheet or iCloud/iOS backups, copies you made there are in your control.

# this website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, cookies or trackers of our own. GitHub, as the host, may keep standard server logs under its own privacy statement. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

# changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
