# the app

Write a question. When it comes up, try to recall the answer before you reveal it, then say how it went. The FSRS-6 scheduler decides when you'll see that card again, so your practice goes where it's needed and nowhere else.

Meet Braino, our brain-karate character. Paper surfaces, ink outlines, warm colours and rotating woodblock artwork give the app its own look in light and dark.

# in the beta candidate

Recall first

Each card asks you to try before you reveal. Rate Forgot, Hard, Good or Easy, with the next interval shown on every button. Undo is one tap away.

FSRS-6 scheduling

Reviews are scheduled by the FSRS-6 algorithm with a 90% retention target, and every review is kept in your history.

Cards your way

Basic, reversed and fill-in-the-blank cards, with images, audio, image masks and offline math equations.

Library and Smart Collections

Nest decks, search questions, answers and tags, and combine filters into saved views.

Bring your material

Import existing decks and CSV files, or draft cards from text, PDFs and photos. Nothing is saved until you approve each card.

Study tools

Read questions and answers aloud, work it out on a scratchpad, or record your voice to compare with a reference.

Belts for showing up

Scheduled reviews earn bricks. Training days earn belts. A rest-day token softens a missed day, and the wisdom scroll collects quotes you've seen. Rewards are optional.

Warm up with Braino

A short, skippable scene before opening a deck or starting study: grass underfoot, boards to break and encouraging thoughts overhead. Reduce Motion uses a still scene.

A changing view

The Today picture fades through 32 woodblock-style scenes in the existing artwork slot. Reduce Motion keeps it still; a switch restores the original print.

Optional iCloud backup

Backup and restore to your own iCloud Drive is built into the candidate and off by default. Live iCloud device testing is still pending. This is backup, not live multi-device sync.

Yours, on your device

Create and study without an account. Cards are saved on the device, with verified backups and snapshots you can restore.
