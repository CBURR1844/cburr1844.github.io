# October 7, 2026 · 1.0 (2)

Uploaded for internal TestFlight processing. This is not an App Store release or an open beta. Upload acceptance does not mean processing or tester access is complete.

The new candidate combines the recent work into one build:

  • Braino warm-ups: a skippable animated scene with a thought cloud, blue mountains and a grassy path.
  • Rotating Today artwork: 32 accepted scenes in the existing picture slot, with slow transitions and a Reduce Motion still.
  • Rewards: bricks for scheduled reviews, belts for training days, a rest-day token and a wisdom scroll of collected quotes.
  • iCloud Drive backup: opt-in backup and restore to the user's own iCloud, off by default. Not live sync; real-device verification remains pending.
  • A clearer tour: small callouts anchored to controls, plus a tab bar that reserves its own space instead of floating over text.

Verified: 163 automated tests pass on the combined candidate. Simulator build, Release archive and distribution signatures passed. Sample-data screenshots show the integrated Today and Progress screens.

Still on the test list: end-to-end live iCloud backup/restore, touch navigation, VoiceOver, iPad layouts and device performance. The rest-token Progress streak can differ from the older strict-streak encouragement message; artwork currently continues rotating beneath a study sheet.

The screenshot gallery uses simulator sample data. The belt celebration is an illustrative milestone, and the warm-up clip is held open for demonstration. These aren't claims of real user progress.
